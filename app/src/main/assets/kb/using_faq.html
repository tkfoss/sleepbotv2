FAQ

  • "Today‘s Sleep" = Your total hours for that day. Entries are tracked by wake time to prevent overlap
  • Where do I put my phone - If motion tracking is on, do not exit the tracking screen, plug your phone in, and place it next to you. (Accelerometer requires the screen to be on!)
    If recording is on, simply make sure to place it where the mic can hear you (like next to your pillow)!
  • Will it work with more than one person - Whether you share the bed with your dog, your baby, or your partner, SleepBot will work if it is placed close enough to your body. Place it on your side of the bed, and near your head or shoulders.
