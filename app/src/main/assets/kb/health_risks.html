Sleep Deprivation Health Problems

Short Term Effects :

  • Aching muscles
  • Dizziness/nausea
  • Headaches
  • Hand/limb tremors
  • Impaired memory
  • Impaired concentration/attentions
  • Impaired hand/eye coordination
  • Involuntary rapid eye movements
  • Increased irritability and stress levels
  • Slower reaction times
  • Increased blood pressure
  • Risk when driving, operating machinery, and doing other activities requiring full functionality (symptoms similar to having blood alcohol content)

Long Term Effects :

  • ADD
  • Depression
  • Decreased ability to heal
  • High blood pressure
  • Increased risk of diabetes
  • Risk of heart attack/stroke
  • Heart disease
  • Obesity
  • Memory Loss
  • Mental/psychiatric problems
  • Sleep attacks (similar to blackouts)
