Entries Tab

Edit daily sleep entries and graphs on this tab.

  1. Date range: View entries or stats within specific dates
  2. Statistics: Includes Average Daily Sleep and Cumulative Sleep Debt based on date range
  3. Quick Delete: Long-press entries
  • Tap graphs to zoom in.
  • Tap sound spikes to play back sounds!

Graphs Tab

Tap to see all graphs and share!
Select time ranges to see your sleep trends.

  1. Current Debt: Hours of sleep you owe over past 10 days, based on your optimal sleep goal. (Positive number = Need more sleep! Negative number = sleeping well)
  2. Graph Types:
    1. Trend - Hours slept per day
    2. Length - Frequency of sleep amounts
    3. Pattern - Time of day you are asleep
    4. Sleep - Frequency of start times
    5. Wake - Frequency of wake times
